This CoreML conversion and Swift integration were published by the
FluidInference team and are based on NVIDIA's Parakeet
(FastConformer-RNNT) model.

Workbench uses the model for private, on-device English speech transcription.
The model weights are downloaded at runtime and are not stored in this
repository.
